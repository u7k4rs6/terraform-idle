Terraform: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Terraform is a free idle game made by Utkarsh Bahuguna. This policy covers the game on the web (utkarshbahuguna.me/terraform-idle), on itch.io and in its Android app.

The short version

Terraform does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your progress (life, worlds, upgrades, talents, achievements) and your settings (music, sound, reduced motion, large text) are saved in your own browser's local storage, on your own device.
  • This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to me or to anyone else.
  • Clearing the app's or the browser's site data deletes it. You can also erase it from Settings, using New game.
  • Settings also offer a save code: a text copy of your progress that you can choose to copy and paste to another device yourself. It is only ever shared if you share it.

What the game does not do

  • No sign-in, accounts or contact details.
  • No advertising, advertising IDs or third-party trackers.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or other device data.
  • No in-app purchases.

Optional features that use your device

  • Photo mode and Share use your device's own share sheet or download. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it.
  • Vibration on supported phones only buzzes the device. It sends nothing anywhere.

Hosting

The game's files are served by GitHub Pages (and by itch.io for the itch version). Like any website host, they may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to deliver the files and keep the service secure. Those logs are controlled by them under their own privacy policies. Terraform itself does not receive or use them.

Children

Terraform does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
